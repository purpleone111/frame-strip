A Video.js 10 skin

Scrub by what you see.

Most players give you a time. Frame Strip gives you the picture. Hover the bar, see real frames from the film, and land on the one you want.

Try it: hover the bar · press Tab · open Display

01 · How it works

Three things to try.

  1. 01

    See

    Hover or focus the seek bar and it opens into a strip of real frames, coloured by chapter, with a larger preview above it.

  2. 02

    Browse

    Frames opens every frame at once, grouped by chapter. Pick one and the film jumps there.

  3. 03

    Adjust

    Display changes how the player looks and moves: four presets, then ten options you can mix. Your choices stay on your device.

02 · Access

Made for everyone.

Accessibility is where this skin gets its ideas. It is the starting point, not an extra.

  • Every control works by keyboard. Tab to move, the arrow keys to scrub, Escape to close a panel.
  • Focus is always visible. A clear ring on every control.
  • Targets of at least 44 px. Bigger controls are one tap away in Display.
  • Colour is never the only cue. Chapters are numbered, spaced apart, and patterned in the colour-blind option.
  • Four presets. Low vision, Colour-blind, Light-sensitive and Easier to press.
  • It listens to your device. Reduced motion, reduced transparency, more contrast and forced colours are followed.
  • Captions when there are captions. The button is always in place, and greyed out when a video has none.
  • Private. What you pick is saved on this device and goes nowhere.

The colour filters are approximations. They are not a medical correction.

03 · Make it yours

Four properties. Nothing else to learn.

Set them on any element around the player.

.my-player {
  --media-accent-color: oklch(0.78 0.14 250);
  --media-accent-text-color: #0a0a0b;
  --media-border-radius: 16px;
  --media-font-family: Inter, system-ui, sans-serif;
}
